# GLACIER — visual analysis of the canonical anime drawing

Written BEFORE sculpt round 12 (guide step 2c): the drawing read as an
explicit, gradeable description. Every build round is graded against the
checklist at the bottom, in writing. B = ground → dome apex (1260 px on the
keyed PNG); axis at x ≈ 615.

## Gestalt

ONE MONOLITHIC MASS. The silhouette is a bell: pauldron line widest
(~0.78 B), sides falling nearly straight to the leg split, legs continuing
the same line to the huge feet. There are NO stacked-segment transitions —
no waist pinch, no pelvis block, no neck. The head, arms and legs read as
things EMBEDDED IN the mass, not things the mass is assembled from. Any
build whose torso reads "chest block + waist + pelvis block" fails the
gestalt regardless of part quality.

## Mass hierarchy (share of the silhouette, rough)

torso+pauldrons ~45% · legs+feet ~35% · lance ~9% · left arm ~6% · head ~4%.
The head is the SMALLEST major element on the figure.

## Regions, measured

1. **HEAD — small and buried.** Dome width 270 px = 0.214 B. Visible height
   above the chest collar only ≈ 0.115 B: the visor band sits practically AT
   the collar line, the chin and jaw are fully hidden inside the torso. The
   crown crystals (to ~0.20 B above the dome) read as part of the BODY's
   crest, not as a hat on a head.
2. **TORSO — flares DOWNWARD.** Widths by row: pauldron line ~0.78 B →
   belly narrowest 0.24 B (y640) → **0.37 B at the leg split** (y720-770).
   The lower torso is the widest part of the core below the chest: a skirt.
   The belly's "narrowing" is gentle and stays PALE — the dark is a shadow
   line, not a dark waist band.
3. **PELVIS — does not exist as a part.** No hip drums, no side skirt
   hardware, no crotch box standing proud. The 0.37 B skirt IS the pelvis;
   a small pale pentagon sits flush at its base. The thighs emerge from
   UNDER it, their tops overlapped/hidden — the transition torso→leg is a
   continuous surface, closer to a gown than a waist.
4. **LEGS.** Each thigh run 0.16-0.18 B wide at the split, holding ~0.15 B
   down the shin; knee high (0.40 B, model-primary); feet paws 0.26 B long.
5. **LANCE ARM — long and slender.** Elbow ~0.67 B high, tip at 0.17 B:
   length ≈ 0.50 B. Shaft diameter ≈ 0.12-0.14 B — LESS THAN HALF the
   pauldron's width, and nearly constant down its length: the segments are
   smooth cylinders with fine panel lines, the collars are THIN rings
   (barely wider than the shaft), the widest point is the elbow cuff at
   ~0.15 B. Nothing on it is chunky; the blades are thin long triangles.
6. **LEFT ARM.** Upper arm ~0.10 B, forearm ~0.11 B, glove ~0.13 B —
   slender against the pauldron above it.
7. **VALUES.** ~80% pale ice-blue. Darks confined to: throat bowl,
   under-pec shadow, the leg-split shadow, joint rings, the glove, soles.
   Glows: visor band (dominant), chest lamp (small), lance core (long).

## Grading checklist (every round, in writing)

- [ ] G1 gestalt: silhouette is one bell-shaped mass; no stacked-block read
- [ ] H1 dome width ≤ 0.22 B; H2 visible head height above collar ≤ 0.12 B
- [ ] T1 no silhouette width below 0.24 B between chest and leg split
- [ ] T2 width at the leg split ≥ 0.34 B (the skirt is the widest core
      element below the chest)
- [ ] P1 no protruding hip hardware (drums, caps, side skirts) in silhouette
- [ ] P2 thigh tops overlapped by the skirt (no gap, no visible joint seam)
- [ ] L1 lance shaft ≤ 0.14 B diameter; L2 collars ≤ 1.15 × shaft;
      L3 length ≥ 0.45 B; L4 widest point is the elbow cuff
- [ ] A1 upper arm ≤ 0.11 B wide
- [ ] F1 feet ≥ 0.24 B long; K1 knee at ~0.40 B (model-primary)

## Round log

- **r11 (graded honestly): FAILS G1, H2, T1, T2, P1, P2, L1, L2.** Dome
  fully visible (~0.18 B showing); dark waist bellows pinch ~0.19 B; pelvis
  a separate faceted block with drums + skirt plates poking the silhouette;
  thigh tops exposed; lance shaft ~0.165 B with fat collars (0.62 r).
- **r12: ALL ROWS PASS.** Dome scaled 0.87× and sunk to the collar (visor
  at the collar line, chin hidden); abdomen pale with thin shadow rings,
  flaring into ONE broad skirt (0.37 B) that the raised thigh lathes tuck
  under; waist rings, hip drums, side-skirt hardware deleted; lance slimmed
  to a 0.115 B shaft with thin collars, cuff the widest point; arms slimmed.
  Remaining nits (logged, not blocking): the skirt could carry more facet
  seams; the crown could rise taller.
